PhD Student

Center for Robotics and Intelligent Systems (CRIS)

Genova, Italy

Pasquale Marra

Research Interests: Force Control, Tactile Sensing, Manipulation.

Bio: Pasquale Marra is a PhD student in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Genoa and Italian Institute of Technology. He is part of the Humanoid Sensing and Perception Lab directed by Lorenzo Natale, and he is supervised by Dr. Gabriele Mario Caddeo. His research focuses on the control of anthropomorphic robotic hands equipped with tactile sensors, with particular interest in grasping, force-aware manipulation, and human–robot collaboration. He has worked on the iCub and ergoCub humanoid platforms, developing control and grasp-planning methods that combine visual and tactile feedback. Pasquale holds bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Automation Engineering from the University of Naples Federico II, both awarded with highest honours.

Education:

  • Ph.D. in Advanced Robotics — University of Genoa, 2028
  • M.Sc./B.Sc. in Automation Engineering — University of Naples Federico II, 2024

Contact: pasquale.marra@iit.it

Center for Robotics and Intelligent Systems (CRIS)

Genova, Italy

Jingchao Hou

Research Interests: Tactile Sensing, Manipulation.

Bio: Jingchao Hou is an M.Sc. student in Mechatronic Engineering at Politecnico di Torino. He received his bachelor’s degree from Shaanxi University of Science & Technology and he is currently conducting his master’s thesis at the Humanoid Sensing and Perception Lab, Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT), under the supervision of Dr. Gabriele Mario Caddeo and Prof. Raffaello Camoriano (Politecnico di Torino). His research focuses on tactile sensing, multimodal perception, and contact-rich robotic manipulation.

Education:

  • M.Sc. in Mechatronic Engineering — Politecnico di Torino, 2026
  • B.Sc. in Mechatronic Engineering — Shaanxi University of Science & Technology, 2023

Contact: jingchao.hou@iit.it
