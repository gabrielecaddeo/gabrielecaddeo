## Pasquale Marra

**Research Interests:** Force Control, Tactile Sensing, Manipulation.

**Bio:** Pasquale Marra is a PhD student in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Genoa and Italian Institute of Technology. He is part of the Humanoid Sensing and Perception Lab directed by Lorenzo Natale, and he is supervised by Dr. Gabriele Mario Caddeo. His research focuses on the control of anthropomorphic robotic hands equipped with tactile sensors, with particular interest in grasping, force-aware manipulation, and human–robot collaboration. He has worked on the iCub and ergoCub humanoid platforms, developing control and grasp-planning methods that combine visual and tactile feedback. Pasquale holds bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Automation Engineering from the University of Naples Federico II, both awarded with highest honours.

**Education:**
