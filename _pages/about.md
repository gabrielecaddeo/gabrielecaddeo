I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence, with a particular interest in developing intelligent systems that can perceive, reason about, and physically interact with the world.

My research focuses on robot perception and manipulation, with an emphasis on visuo-tactile sensing and multimodal learning. I am also interested in generative models and their application to robotics, particularly for learning complex representations of objects, interactions, and physical environments.

My work combines modern learning-based approaches with classical control methods. More broadly, my goal is to develop robotic systems that can understand physical interactions and operate reliably in complex, contact-rich environments. Recently, I have been focusing on the development of tactile and haptic systems, as well as tactile signal generation from human videos.

Alongside my research, I am also interested in supporting the robotics community and creating opportunities for early-career researchers to share their work and exchange ideas.
